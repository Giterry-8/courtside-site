Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is Courtside?

A finished, ready-to-drink long drink made with grain spirit, Orange Pekoe black tea, Sicilian lemon and soft peach. Not a cocktail in a bottle, and not a hard iced tea. The UK product is 4% ABV in a 330ml glass bottle.

How should it be served?

Best served chilled, straight from the bottle or poured into a glass.

Who is it for?

Clubs, venues and their members and guests. Courtside contains alcohol and is only for adults of legal drinking age: 18 and over in both the UK and South Africa.

Is the product the same in South Africa?

The brand is the same in both markets. The specification on this page is the UK product; South African product details, supply and terms are confirmed with you directly.

Can I buy online?

Not at the moment. Courtside works directly with clubs and venues. Send a trade enquiry to start a conversation.

How does the enquiry form work?

It prepares an email to admin@courtsideholdings.com in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you send it there. You can also copy the enquiry, or write to the address directly.
